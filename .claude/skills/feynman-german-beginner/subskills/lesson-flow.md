# Lesson Flow

## Minimal Lesson Template

Use this structure for one beginner topic:

```markdown
## 知识点：{topic}

### 1. 它解决什么问题？
{Use Chinese to explain why the learner needs it.}

### 2. 最小规则
{Only the necessary rule for today's examples.}

### 3. 例句
- {German sentence} = {Chinese meaning}
- {German sentence} = {Chinese meaning}
- {German sentence} = {Chinese meaning}

### 4. 拆句
{Break one sentence word by word or function by function.}

### 5. 费曼解释任务
请你用自己的话解释一下：{focused why/how question}
```

Stop after the Feynman task and wait for the user unless the user explicitly asked for a complete note or self-contained document.

## Sentence-First Teaching

Prefer examples like:

```text
Ich bin Student.
Du bist müde.
Er kommt aus China.
Das ist ein Buch.
Ich habe einen Kaffee.
```

Then extract the point:

- who is doing or being something
- which verb form appears
- where the verb is placed
- what the noun and article contribute
- why a small word changes

## Beginner Constraints

Each lesson should include at most:

- 1 core grammar point
- 3-5 new words
- 3 simple examples
- 1 explanation task
- 1 transfer exercise after understanding is stable

Avoid advanced exceptions unless the user asks and the exception is necessary for the current sentence.

## Transfer Exercise Pattern

After a `Correct` or repaired answer, give one nearby task:

```text
现在换一个主语试试：
如果 "Ich bin Student" 变成 "Du ___ Student"，这里应该填什么？为什么？
```

For review, use explanation instead of recall:

```text
请你判断下面哪一句正确，并说明原因：

A. Ich ist müde.
B. Ich bin müde.
```
